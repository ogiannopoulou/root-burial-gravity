{\Large\textbf{Highlights}}\par
\vspace{1em}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Morphoelastic-rod model of root growth into a Mohr-Coulomb granular medium
  \item Gravity enters via self-weight, granular strength, and statolith gain
  \item Exact penetration law: burial depth scales inversely with gravity ($z^{*}\sim 1/g$)
  \item Numerics confirm the law over a 30-fold range of gravitational acceleration
  \item Gravitropism and circumnutation act as independent depth and shape controls
\end{itemize}
